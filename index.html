Stay balanced

Know where the hours go

See hours by subject for each kid, every week and month. Spot the subject that's falling behind before the end of the year sneaks up on you.

Be ready

Your year-end report, done

Total hours, days attended and starred work samples are tallied as you go. When it's time to report, preview, print or share in one tap, or export a spreadsheet.

Look organized

A clean record you can hand to anyone

The PDF report covers hours by subject and month, an attendance calendar, field trips and work samples, with a line to sign. It's built around the records most states ask you to keep.

Everything a homeschool record needs

One-tap timers

Start, pause and finish a subject in a tap, or add time afterward.

Every kid, their own way

Each child gets an avatar, their own subjects and their own streak.

Field trips and events

Plan co-op days and outings, get reminders, and log them with photos and voice notes.

Portfolio

Photos of their work, labeled by subject. Star the best for the report.

Year-end PDF

Hours by subject and month, attendance, field trips and work samples.

Catch up mid-year

Import a spreadsheet, snap a photo of a paper log, or enter totals.

Private by design

Your kids' records never leave your family. There's no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Records stay on your devices and back up to your own iCloud. We never see them.
