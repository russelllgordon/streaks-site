Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Streaks works right away. If you're signed in to iCloud, your records sync across your own iPhone and iPad automatically.

Does Streaks make sure I meet my state's rules?

No. Streaks helps you keep good records and gives some states a starting goal, but rules change and vary. Please check your state's current homeschool requirements.

I'm starting partway through the year.

Go to Settings (the gear), then Catch up on this school year. You can import a spreadsheet (CSV), snap a photo of a paper log, or enter totals by subject. Every import shows a preview first and can be undone under Settings › Imports.

What should my spreadsheet look like?

A CSV with a header row containing Date, Subject, and Minutes or Hours, plus an optional Note column. Columns can be in any order.

How do I make the year-end report?

Open the Reports tab, choose the dates, and tap Year-end PDF report. Star photos in the Portfolio to include them as work samples.

I started a timer by accident.

On the timer screen, tap Discard. Short runs under 10 seconds are dropped automatically.

How do I remove a kid or a subject?

Open Settings and swipe left on the kid or subject. Hiding a subject keeps its hours in your reports; deleting removes them.

Reminders or voice notes aren't working.

Check the Settings app › Streaks and make sure Notifications, Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed.
