Partner with Streaks

Streaks: Homeschool Hours is a simple iPhone and iPad app for logging learning time and making clean reports. We'd love to help your families keep better records with less work.

Built for ESA and school-choice platforms, charter and enrollment programs, and curriculum providers whose families need to show what they did and when.

Why it helps your families

ESA-ready hour records

Every entry has a date, a kid, a subject and minutes, plus optional notes and photos. Records are organized and easy to hand over when a program asks for documentation.

State-aware reports

A year-end PDF with hours by subject and month, attendance days, field trips and work samples, plus CSV export. Several states have a built-in starting goal.

Logging in seconds

Tap a subject to start a timer, or add time afterward. Families who fall behind can catch up by importing a spreadsheet or a photo of a paper log.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Records stay on the family's devices and their own iCloud. No server holds kids' data.

Streaks helps families keep good records. It does not guarantee that any record or report meets a state's or program's requirements, so families should still check the current rules that apply to them.
